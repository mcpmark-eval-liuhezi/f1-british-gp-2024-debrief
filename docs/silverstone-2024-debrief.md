# Debrief: The 2024 British Grand Prix at Silverstone

*A fact-checked anniversary look back at Round 12 of the 2024 FIA Formula One World Championship.*

## The Race

On Sunday, 7 July 2024, Lewis Hamilton won the British Grand Prix at Silverstone Circuit in a rain-affected thriller, taking his ninth home victory and the 104th win of his Formula 1 career. Starting from second on the grid in car number 44, Hamilton crossed the line 1.465 seconds ahead of Max Verstappen after a late-race charge on slick tyres.

## Methodology

Race, session and lap data in this piece was pulled from the F1 data APIs; as the FastF1 project notes in discussion #517, lap data may include a small amount of generated or interpolated content when pulling historical season and lap data, so lap-level numbers are treated as needing a second independent source before publication.

## Data Sources

- Winner and podium: race results data, corroborated by contemporaneous news reports (RacingNews365, PlanetF1, Wikipedia).
- Driver profile (car number, nationality): driver record and race result data agree.
- Weekend session schedule: two independent session records agree.
- Lap-level numbers and championship standings: single-source so far, flagged for a second check.
